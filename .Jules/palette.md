## 2025-04-25 - Modal Component Accessibility Enhancements
**Learning:** Found that the custom Modal component was lacking critical screen reader attributes and focus states for keyboard users. While a close button existed, it was just an icon with no `aria-label`, meaning screen readers would read it poorly. The entire component lacked the `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"` properties.
**Action:** When implementing or updating custom overlay components, ensure that React's `useId()` is leveraged to tie the `aria-labelledby` directly to the `id` of the modal title. Add `focus-visible` styling (`focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary-500`) to non-standard interactive elements like icon-only close buttons. Add `aria-label` to these buttons and `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative SVGs.
