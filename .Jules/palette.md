## 2024-05-24 - Explicit ARIA Boundaries for Custom Modals
**Learning:** Custom modals in this Next.js app lacked screen reader support and explicit ARIA boundaries. Because native `<dialog>` isn't being used, we must explicitly declare `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` on the modal container, and provide a corresponding `id` on the heading, to ensure screen readers properly announce the modal and focus context. Additionally, icon-only buttons (like the Close "X") required `aria-label` and `focus-visible` styles for basic accessibility.
**Action:** Always verify that custom overlay components (modals, drawers, popovers) in this application include standard WAI-ARIA dialog attributes and that their structural elements (headings, close buttons) are correctly linked and labeled.
