## 2025-04-17 - Accessible Custom Modals
**Learning:** When implementing custom modals in this Next.js 14 design system, standard accessible attributes (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`) were missing. Additionally, dynamic association of titles to modals requires strictly using React 18's `useId()` (e.g. `const titleId = useId()`) instead of manual or random IDs to prevent server-side hydration mismatches, while icon-only close buttons require explicit `aria-label` definitions and hidden SVG paths to prevent screen reader clutter.
**Action:** Always wrap custom overlay dialogs with `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`. Generate unique accessibility IDs unconditionally with `useId()` inside client components, ensuring target text (like title headings) correctly associates with `aria-labelledby`. Hide decorative SVGs and provide explicit text labels on icon-only interactive elements.
