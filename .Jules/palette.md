## 2024-05-16 - Modal Dialog Accessibility
**Learning:** Reusable modal components must properly announce themselves as dialogs to screen readers and correctly handle accessible names for icon-only close buttons. Without `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`, screen readers might not confine reading to the modal contents. Furthermore, icon-only close buttons require an explicit `aria-label` and visible focus states (like `focus-visible:ring-2`) to be fully accessible for keyboard and screen reader users.
**Action:** Always wrap modal/dialog components with `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and link the heading with `aria-labelledby`. Ensure all icon-only buttons include an `aria-label`, hide the internal SVGs with `aria-hidden="true"`, and provide explicit `focus-visible` styling for keyboard users.
